About

Before I built AI systems, I built precision. Seven-plus years supervising high-access construction and safety-critical projects taught me one thing: systems either work exactly right, or they fail — there's no in-between. I bring that same engineering discipline to AI-driven growth. I'm not a typical freelancer stitching together templates — I design and build the actual systems (websites, content pipelines, AI automation) that local businesses need to compete and grow, with the same precision I'd apply to a project where mistakes aren't an option.

How I work

Four steps, no mystery.

  1. Discover

    We start with the problem, not the pixels. What the business needs, who it's for, and what "working" actually looks like.

  2. Design

    Structure and look decided before a line of code — layout, hierarchy, and how the whole thing should feel to use.

  3. Build

    Built for real conditions: fast, accessible, and responsive on every screen — not just the one it was designed on.

  4. Launch

    Shipped, checked, and handed over — with everything set up so it stays easy to update after I'm gone.
